# Getting Started

## What is JoAi?

JoAi is a platform for creating, managing, and interacting with AI agents that automate tasks, manage digital assets, and connect with various services. Agents are intelligent assistants that work 24/7 to help you save time and maximize productivity.

## Key Concepts

- **Agents** - AI assistants that perform tasks and manage assets
- **Swarms** - Groups of agents working together
- **Teams** - Collaborative workspaces for multiple users
- **Wallets** - Blockchain wallets for managing digital assets
- **Blueprints** - Pre-configured agent templates
- **Integrations** - Connections to external services

## Onboarding

When you first sign in, you'll complete a brief onboarding:

1. **Welcome** - Introduction to JoAi
2. **Authentication** - Complete account setup (email, social login, or wallet)
3. **First Agent** - Create or select your first agent

After onboarding, you'll be taken to your home dashboard where you can start interacting with agents.
