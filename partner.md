## Partnership Overview

### What You Get

As a JoAi partner, you receive:

- **Documentation Exposure**: Your integration listed in our partner documentation with backlinks
- **Dedicated Partner Page**: Custom page at `joai.ai/partners/[your-slug]` showcasing your integration
- **Co-Marketing Opportunities**: Joint blog posts, social media campaigns, and press releases
- **Technical Support**: Priority access to our integration team and technical resources
- **Custom Warps**: Pre-built automation workflows tailored to your users' needs
- **Badge Assets**: Professional "Powered by JoAi Warps" badges for your website and marketing materials
- **SEO Benefits**: Integration pages and documentation that drive organic traffic

### What We Expect

To maintain an active partnership, we expect:

- **Integration Implementation**: Working integration using one of our technical entry points (API, MCP, or smart contracts)
- **Documentation Mention**: Reference to JoAi Warps in your documentation or user-facing UI
- **Distribution**: At least one distribution channel activated (see [Distribution Opportunities](#distribution-opportunities) below)
- **Ongoing Engagement**: Regular communication about integration updates and user feedback

### Partnership Categories

We work with partners across different categories:

- **API & Infrastructure Tools**: Email services, analytics, storage, authentication, AI APIs
- **Apps & SaaS Platforms**: CRMs, automation tools, dashboards, productivity apps
- **DeFi & Blockchain Apps**: Lending platforms, staking services, DEXs, yield aggregators
- **Foundations & Ecosystems**: Blockchain foundations and ecosystem organizations

Each category has specific integration patterns and distribution opportunities tailored to your platform type.

### Technical Integration Options

Choose the integration method that best fits your platform:

**1. REST API**

- Programmatic access to JoAi Warps
- Webhook support for real-time events
- Full SDK available for custom integrations
- Best for: Backend services, automation tools, CRMs

**2. MCP (Model Context Protocol)**

- Native protocol integration for AI agents
- Seamless agent-to-agent communication
- Best for: AI tools, agent platforms, developer tools

**3. Smart Contract Integration**

- Direct on-chain integration
- Smart contract calls and interactions
- Best for: DeFi protocols, blockchain-native apps, DAOs

**4. Embedded UI**

- Badge integration (see [One-Click Embed Code](#one-click-embed-code))
- Partner page linking
- Best for: All platforms (quickest to implement)

### Distribution Opportunities

Every partnership should activate at least one distribution channel:

**1. Documentation Integration**

- Add JoAi Warps to your integration docs
- Include setup guides and use cases
- Link to your partner page

**2. Integration Page**

- Create a dedicated page showcasing the integration
- Include examples: "YourProduct + JoAi Warps = [Outcome]"
- SEO-optimized content

**3. "Built with" Badge**

- Display "Powered by JoAi Warps" badge on your site
- Link to your partner page with proper tracking

**4. Blog Post / Tutorial**

- Co-authored content about the integration
- Use case examples and tutorials
- Cross-promotion on both platforms

**5. Template / Preset**

- Pre-configured Warps using your product
- Shareable templates for your users
- Featured in our Warp marketplace

### Partnership Success

Successful partnerships demonstrate:

- **User Value**: Clear benefit to your users (time saved, errors reduced, capabilities expanded)
- **Mutual Growth**: Both platforms see increased engagement and adoption
- **Long-term Commitment**: Ongoing collaboration and feature development
- **Community Impact**: Positive feedback from users of both platforms

Ready to become a partner? Here's the process:

4. **Launch**: Co-announce the partnership with marketing materials and documentation

5. **Ongoing Support**: Regular check-ins and collaboration on new features

### Design Guidelines

**Visual Design Elements:**

- **Color Palette**: Primary: Neon purple (#A855F7 / #9333EA), Accent: Electric blue (#3B82F6), Background: Transparent or subtle gradient, Text: White (dark badges) / Dark gray (light badges)
- **Typography**: Font: Clean sans-serif (Mona Sans or similar), Weight: Medium to Semi-bold, Size: Optimized for readability at specified dimensions
- **Icon**: Small agent/robot icon positioned to the left of text, Minimalist, modern design, Matches MultiversX ecosystem aesthetic
- **Layout**: Horizontal badges: Icon + "Powered by JoAi Warps" text, Square badges: Centered icon + text stack

**Usage Recommendations:**

1. Horizontal badges: Best for footers, headers, and inline content
2. Square badges: Ideal for social media, app stores, and profile pages
3. Always link badges to your JoAi Warps page with proper UTM tracking
4. Maintain aspect ratio when resizing (use CSS to constrain dimensions)
5. Use appropriate variant based on background color for optimal contrast

**Brand Guidelines:**

- Do not modify badge colors, fonts, or layout
- Maintain minimum clear space (20% of badge height) around badge
- Do not stretch or distort badge proportions
- Ensure sufficient contrast with background (WCAG AA compliant)

- **Badge not showing?** Check that you replaced `[PARTNER_SLUG]`, verify image URLs are accessible
